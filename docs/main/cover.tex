We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled
\emph{Estimating duration in sex work from cross-sectional studies:
      a Bayesian global synthesis suggests shorter durations}
for consideration to publish as a \emph{Practice of Epidemiology} article in \emph{AJE}.
\par
We were first motivated to quantify durations in sex work
to use as inputs to HIV transmission models.
However, the most recent systematic review of mean durations in sex work
applied three estimation methods which we found
to require data that were rarely available, or
to likely produce biased estimates by ignoring key sampling issues.
We thus wrote this manuscript to derive the exact relationship between
commonly available right-censored durations from cross-sectional studies and
true total durations in sex work overall.
Through a Bayesian model of this relationship, we estimated that
true mean durations are at least 2 times shorter than previous estimates, and that
upwards of half of women selling sex do so for less than 1 year.
\par
These findings have two major implications.
First, shorter mean duration in sex work suggests that incidence of HIV and possibly other STI
could be higher than previously thought, to match the same HIV/STI prevalence data in sex work.
Second, maximizing program and service coverage during early sex work
could be highly efficient at reducing population-level HIV/STI transmission,
since many women may exit sex work before programs/services reach them.
\par
While we focus on duration in sex work as a salient example,
our methods and insights are applicable to characterizing the distribution of durations in
any transient health state or period of exposure to risk/protective factors.
Therefore, we expect our paper will be of interest to the broad readership of \emph{AJE},
and we have tried to use simple/accessible language and intuitive visualizations throughout.
\par
We have no competing interests to declare, and
we have not used AI for any part of this work.
We used only published aggregate data, and
our complete analysis code is available online.
