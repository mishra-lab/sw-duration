\paragraph{Objectives}
Our objectives were to:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \label{obj:1}
        Describe the analytic relationship between
        the distribution of reported durations selling sex in observational studies, and
        the distribution of total durations selling sex in the population overall.
  \item \label{obj:2}
        Develop and apply a Bayesian model which leverages the above relationships to infer
        the distribution of total durations selling sex in the population overall
        from published data.
\end{enumerate}
While we focus on sex work as an illustrative example,
our insights extend to any transient risk/disease state,
relevant to compartmental modelling or epidemiological study.
Indeed, similar work has previously examined estimation of
disease state "sojourn times" in models of chronic diseases \cite{Zelen1969}.
