Estimating duration in sex work from cross-sectional studies:
Bayesian global synthesis suggests shorter durations
------------------------------------------------------------

Mean duration in sex work is a key input to HIV/STI transmission models,
typically estimated from cross-sectional studies.
However, existing estimates have not leveraged
the exact relationship between the distributions of
censored durations reported by active sex workers, and
total durations among sex workers overall.
In this talk, we derive this relationship conceptually and mathematically.
Then, using a Bayesian model of the relationship
with aggregate data from 58,249 sex workers globally,
we estimate mean durations by region.
Our estimates are significantly shorter than previous estimates,
and we discuss the implications.
